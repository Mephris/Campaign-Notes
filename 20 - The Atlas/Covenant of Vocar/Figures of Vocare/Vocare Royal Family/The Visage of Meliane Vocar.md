## Visage of Meliane Vocar
The remembrance of a tragically murdered princess of the [[Vocar]] family. A year after marrying a young talent of [[Lune]] family. 

### Life of Meliane Vocar 
The 4th princess of the Vocar family, widely feared by the nobility for her vengeful calm personality and capability in widely feared flesh-warping. 

In her teens, Meliane Vocar has openly shown support for the first prince claim for the throne and taken the high society of the capital by storm as a silent flesh-warper, after her healing the mutilated face of an [[Lune]] family official. 

In the next years, she has been quietly growing in skills, her artworks laid openly in the houses of her supporters, together with the beautiful faces they bore. 

In her twenties she lost her voice due to MTD, yet her appearance always remained free of the visual parts of the disease. 

She was known to openly worship the Fae Queen, Danu, and strived to make her face the closest to her dreamy visage. 

Meliane Vocar was found dead in her room, laid on the black sheeted bed, with violet flowers around her body and crystal growths devouring her flesh, while [[Lune]] family announced her death as a result of flesh warping spell gone wrong, the royal family openly admonished the claim due to Meliane's skill. 

### Blue Queen Danann / Visage of Meliane Vocar
After death of Meliane, her husband disappeared for years, often thought to be the reason of her death. Later on he was found in old [[Lune]] mansion, turned to crystal much like his late wife. In his lab was found a [[Doll]] made in her honor. 

#### Visage of Meliane Vocar
The perfect copy of Meliane Vocar, having foggy recollections of her past, sharing the quiet personality Meliane was "Loved" for as well as her hobbies, her paintings and sculptures often show the night skies and dreamy shapes. 

After being placed in the Grand Royal Theatre, she has taken the crowd before the show by a beautiful song, leaving those that heard it stunned for minutes after. 

The followers of Beauty, believe that Meliane succeeded in taking the form of the Fae Queen enough to gain a fraction of her voice. 

#### Blue Queen Danann
The more widely spread name of the visage, due to it being widely used by the followers of fae cultures, cults or religions. 

While in the country of her origin, and by more common sensed folk she is seen as an item, the more fervent cultists treat her as an item of worship... or a vessel possesed by the queen herself. 

It is not known if Visage of Meliane has any connection to the Fae Queen... however the sudden skill of singing, the quirks beneath the naked night skies... and the disappearances surrounding the visage are hard to argue with. 

#### Doll Experiment 013, The Hag Doll
The act of crafting the visage does not make sense whenever put into time frame, the doll being found days after release of the official Doll technology by the Lune family. 
However people are smarter then to pry into it. 

The crystalized corpse of the princess shortly after its burial was dug out by none other then her husband, under direct orders of an Lune Magus. To take flesh for an experiment of revival, the flesh was turned and sculpted into the inner mechanisms and magic machinery of the doll, while the outer shell was made of a spell resistant porcelain. The appearance was made using Meliane's research to the appearance of the Fae Queen. 

While the doll was made, Lune warriors has managed to capture a fleeting soul of a long running rampant hag. The spells on the shell were strict as with any doll, the soul of a hag was surprisingly plaint to the modifications. 

The process was going well, until night of death of Meliane's husband. Who sealed the laboratory in order to finish the work with his wife's soul instead. 

*It is only theorized what happened inside, for what public doesn't know is that the laboratory once opened.* Had a kneeling crysalized corpse of the lone scientist surrounded by soft necromantic energies, a doll with clear memories of Meliane Vocar, parts such as life-like hair, organic nails, tongue and internal organs, predatory sharp teeth akin to the hags, as well as a missing Hag Soul. 

### Mysteries
After showcase of the Visage, many accidental or coincedental things keep on happening. 

#### Cults
Many [[Beauty]] cults keep on appearing in the capital, at first it was connected to the visage however the movements of cults is strictly avoidant to the Grand Royal Theatre, it is not known if its out of reverence or fear. 

#### Dreams and Songs 
People who hear the song of the Blue Queen seem to sleep deeply on the same night, often oversleeping which led to people taking longer vacations just for the act of hearing the Doll-song. 

Those few that fall asleep for long enough after Doll-song, wake up looking a little different, as if visited by an aesthetic fleshwarper. 

Additionally the people previously operated on by Meliane, have not aged a single day in the last decade. 

#### Visage weird placements. 
The Doll, visage, is at this point commonly known to wander the Grand Theatre, at times going missing for short periods of time only to reappear days after with a bouquet of flowers. 

Once asked about the flowers or its wearabouts during the missing time, the Visage simply answered it was visiting its other bed.
