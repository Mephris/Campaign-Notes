**One horror hook I'd flag rather than assume you want:** does full crystallization always mean the person's just gone — inert ore now — or can some of them retain enough coherence to end up as one of those "arcane souls between spirit and physical" you mentioned earlier, except now trapped inside a crystal that's been mined, sold, and is currently sitting in some noble's enchanted jewelry or a mage's spell-focus? Could be a single gutting reveal rather than a running plot thread if you don't want to overuse it, but it's sitting right there in what you've already built.
