
### The Dogma within Sands
> [!quote] Candella Tyrian
> After long march through the frozen glass ocean, in his last century, The first Tyrian, Candella stopped on the evershifting sands of [[Placeholder Desert Name]]. Pondering how to pass on his philosophy onto his children once no word remains of him. 
> In scarred hand he grasped onto the sand, wet with the last waters of drying oasis. 
> *"My children, those sands will be my words to you. 
> Shift like the sands beneath the feet of your ancestors, 
> Everlook for the next oasis, when your last one dries, 
> Run, hide or face your brothers, as you would the skyflame.*

### Philosophy

The centerpiece of Candella's teachings was the philosophy of movement and growth. Of always moving beyond what you know in search of a better tomorrow. 

After death of Candella, the aelf following his philosophy always moved on, not being able to create a stable governmental structure. Creating a tight working system of a nation shifting like the sands they walk on.
