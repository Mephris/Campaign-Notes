The land holding the delicately balanced country of [[Móraelf]] following the teachings of [[Candella]]. 

The long spanning [[Placeholder Desert Name]] filled with buildings akin to ruins and evermoving clans that never settle in one place for too long. 

The [[Desert Dogma of Tyrian]] does not have a direct center leader, instead they have a delicate balance between its clans, working together and ever-growing together. 

While it is the oldest living nation on the maps, the [[Desert Dogma of Tyrian]] did not expand beyond its deserts. The culture of inner growth over the creation of lasting political infrastructure and ignorance of bloodline culture limited the growth of nature to a developed land of people that still work like small societies would, in tribes. 


### Ruin-dens
The desert holds many sites, constantly abandoned and re-visited by the different tribes. The "dens" as they are called can vary in size depending on the age it was created in, many different tribes can take housing in the same den but different parts of it depending on the size of the den. 

Some dens have sizes of cities, the capital being one of such, with many tribes taking shelter, each tribe holds a different district to avoid conflicts. If any arise they are solved either with mediation between clans by the [[Desert Clans#Running from Skyflame, The Couriers & Bards]] or if both clans are of the same type by duels in their specific fields (melee for warriors, magic for hiding)

### The title of Tyrian Dreadnaught
Among the [[Desert Dogma of Tyrian]] there is a single title held by the strongest warrior, title of a Dreadnaught is passed whenever a warrior kills their predecessor or wins the grand melee. 

The first Dreadnaught is [[Candella]], The Dreadnaught of Looms Gullet.
