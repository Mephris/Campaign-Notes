### Traits
#### Lineage: Sith Pureblood
> [!tip] ☉ Sith Blood (Force).
> You read and speak the Sith language. Sith holocrons, tombs and wards recognise you as an heir. They open for you, and the spirits that guard them will at least speak with you before they strike.

> [!tip] Ancient Power (Force) (Req: Sith Blood)
>  When you mark Strain to reroll a die on a Force ability roll, reroll every blank die in the rolled dice pool instead.
#### Taken
> [!tip] **Force Heal (Action, Force). Presence + Force, T-2.**
Clear 1 Wound from a character in medium range. Each Edge clears one more Wound. You also satisfy the trained-hand requirement for a breather. Does not work on droids.

>[!tip] **Lightsaber Training (Passive, Force).**
> Your lightsaber attacks use Force instead of Technique. You may also throw a one-handed lightsaber as a ranged attack, rolling Vigor + Force. It keeps its Move and returns to your hand after the throw.
