Daughter of [[Lune Father]] & [[Lune Mother]], closed in the deeper archives. Original creator of Magic. 

Life: [[3 - Age of Miracles - Life & Ambition]].

[[Magia]] was sealed within the old archives due to her condition which made her see the things that did not exist, spending most of her time researching the newly appeared mix of essences from the fall of [[Valka]].

Her work began as a way to make her friends real, giving raise to the first familiars. In the end her work has been taken away by her parents and family, used to overthrow the [[Vocare Kingdom.canvas]].
