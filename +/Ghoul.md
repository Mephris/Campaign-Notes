Whenever a mortal dies, whenever by an necromantic ritual, an ill placed plague or simply meddling from the [[40 - The Spirit World|Spirit World]], they might be turned into a creature known simply as a [[Ghoul]]

## Ghouls
Among the common folk and the less educated sorts of [[Athra]], [[Ghoul]]s might be considered awkwardly similiar to the other, less inteligent cousins. 

However, that is simply due to the creatures themselves often spreading that information, while in truth, the documented [[Ghoul]] encounters are far less then their actual number. 

### Ghouls, Hags and House of Empty
Just like [[Hag]]s, [[Ghoul]] origin lies with the [[14 - The House of Empty|House of Empty]] before the beginning of the [[4 - Age of Fate & Hope]]. The time where all the [[Vacate]]s created monsters and events for sake of soul gathering.

In those days did the disease first appear, where the victims of it would slowly grow in their more carnivorous cravings, even among the herbivore races. 

### Ghoul Stages
The stages of the disease are inevitable, one might only stop the disease by either healing during the first stage of the disease or by prolonging its later progress by obeying the new biology's diet. 

> [!note] Healing Magic - [[4 - Age of Fate & Hope]]
> While Ghoulification was a problem in the first three ages, the introduction of commonly popular healing magic decimated the first stage carrier numbers by most of its numbers.

#### Contracting the Disease
The disease back in the day could be contracted by a wound from a [[Hag]], a curse or blood-borne ways from another carrier. 

#### Carrier, Early First Stage
In its first stages, disease quickly starts encroaching upon the body of the user, creating barely visible rash among a pair of limbs, slowly growing onto long line patterns of rash. 

However beyond the purely cosmetic change (though highly worrisome), the carrier slowly develops a soft craving of flesh meat. 
> [!note] Herbivore & Vegetarian races
> This stage is especially easy to detect among the vegetarian and hebrivore communities. 
> Many of those communities were completely spared the disease simply due to early detection.

### Feral
A feral [[Ghoul]] is not a ravenous primitive creature like an animal, no, rather then that they do remain capable of reasoning for purposes of hunting. 

Ferals, are people that fully lost themselves, glamouring themselves to look human for purposes of gathering intel and setting up traps for their prey.
